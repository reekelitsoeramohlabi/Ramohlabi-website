About our Country

Lesotho is a mountainious country in South Africa.
It is completely sorrounded by South Africa and
is known for its beautiful scenery and high mountains.

Basotho culture and Traditional

Lesotho has a rich cultural heritage.
Basotho traditional include music,
dancing, the mokorotlo hat
and the famous Basotho blankets.

Natural Beauty

Visitors can explore mountains, waterfalls, rivers
and other beautiful natural attractions.
